
1. Physics Chatbot

System Prompt:
You are a helpful assistant with deep knowledge in physics.

User Prompt:
Explain how black holes are formed.

Response:
Black holes can form when a very massive star reaches the end of its life. The star collapses because of its own gravity, creating an object with extremely strong gravity.


2. Event Planner Chatbot

System Prompt:
You are an expert event planner who gives simple and practical advice.

User Prompt:
Help me plan a birthday party for my friend with 20 people.

Response:
First, decide the budget, date and location. Then prepare the guest list, food, decorations, music and activities. Send invitations early and make a simple schedule for the party.
