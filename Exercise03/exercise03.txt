Report

I asked all three LLMs:

“How can I use generative AI as a university student to learn Probability and Statistics, Database Management Systems, and Software Engineering?”

The tools explained that generative AI can help me in different ways.

For Probability and Statistics, AI can explain formulas in simple words, solve problems step by step, create practice questions, and explain mistakes.

For Database Management Systems, AI can help me understand SQL queries, database design, normalization, ER diagrams, and give examples of CRUD operations.

For Software Engineering, AI can explain concepts such as requirements, use case diagrams, software development models, testing, and also help me understand code.

I also found that AI can create quizzes, summaries, examples, and revision questions, which can make studying easier and faster.


My Reflection

I found all three tools useful. DeepSeek R1 gave detailed explanations, Qwen Thinking explained concepts clearly, and Grok DeepSearch gave more detailed information by searching for relevant information.

In my opinion, Grok DeepSearch produced the best results because the answers were detailed and included more useful information. However, I should still check important information instead of depending completely on AI.
