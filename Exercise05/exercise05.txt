12-13. My Selected Natural Language Model

I selected:
Model: distilbert-base-uncased-finetuned-sst-2-english
This model can identify whether text has a positive or negative sentiment.
from transformers import pipeline


classifier = pipeline(
    "sentiment-analysis",
    model="distilbert-base-uncased-finetuned-sst-2-english"
)

result = classifier("I really enjoyed learning about artificial intelligence.")
print(result)
